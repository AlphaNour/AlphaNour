### Tips
- Use a Canadian-format CV (no photo, no age/marital status, 2 pages max) and get your diploma evaluated by **WES** (https://www.wes.org/ca).
- French is a strong asset: Express Entry French-language draws + bilingual roles in Ottawa.
- Prepare English test (IELTS General / CELPIP) and French (TEF Canada) — French scores give big CRS bonus points.
- Network: Supply Chain Canada Ontario chapter, LinkedIn groups, Algerian community associations in Montréal/Toronto.

### Neighbourhoods to consider (for work)
- **Bab Ezzouar / Dar El Beïda** — near airport, business district, USTHB; many logistics, freight & pharma companies; reasonable rents.
- **Hydra / Ben Aknoun / Saïd Hamdine** — corporate offices, multinationals; expensive.
- **Kouba / Hussein Dey / Bir Mourad Raïs** — central, mid-priced.
- **Rouiba / Réghaïa** — industrial zone (manufacturing, logistics warehouses); cheapest.
- **Chéraga / Ouled Fayet / Dely Ibrahim** — west side, newer buildings, pharma companies (Saidal, Hikma, Biopharm area).

Rough monthly rent (verify on Ouedkniss): shared room/colocation ~15,000–30,000 DZD; F2 ~40,000–70,000 DZD; F3 ~60,000–120,000 DZD (Hydra higher). Landlords often ask for 6–12 months paid upfront — negotiate monthly or 3 months.

### Practical tips
- Never send money before visiting and seeing the owner's ID + livret foncier / acte.
- Sign a contract at a notaire or with an agence immobilière agréée.
- Travel Tlemcen → Alger: Air Algérie (Zenata airport, ~1h), train SNTF (~10h), or bus.
