## 3. Housing in Algiers
| Site | Use |
|---|---|
| https://www.ouedkniss.com | Immobilier > Location / Colocation, wilaya Alger |
| Facebook Marketplace + groups | "Colocation Alger", "Chambre à louer Alger" |
| https://www.lkeria.com, https://www.immobilier-dz.com | Rental listings |
| Airbnb / Booking.com | First 1–3 weeks while interviewing & visiting |

Best areas by job location:
- **Bab Ezzouar / Dar El Beïda / Oued Smar** — logistics, airport, Biopharm, forwarders.
- **Rouiba / Réghaïa** — industrial zone, cheapest rents.
- **Hydra / Ben Aknoun / Saïd Hamdine** — head offices, expensive.
- **El Harrach / Kouba / Hussein Dey** — central, Saidal, mid-price.
- **Chéraga / Ouled Fayet** — pharma & new offices, west side.

Rough monthly rents (estimates — check Ouedkniss): colocation 15,000–30,000 DZD · F2 40,000–70,000 DZD · F3 60,000–120,000 DZD. Negotiate against 6–12 months upfront; visit first, check owner ID + documents, sign at a notaire or licensed agency.

## 4. Plan from Tlemcen
1. Update CV (French, 1–2 pages) + LinkedIn headline "Procurement & Supply Chain Engineer".
2. Create alerts on Emploitic + LinkedIn; apply 5–10 per day.
3. Group interviews into one trip to Algiers (Air Algérie from Zenata ~1h, or train/bus); stay in a short-term rental.
4. Once an offer is signed, rent near the workplace.
