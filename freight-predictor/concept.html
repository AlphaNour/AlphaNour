    Where the gap probably is

    Freight tools answer "what does the box cost?" Nobody answers "what does this deal cost me?"

    What existsWhat it misses for our buyer
    Freight marketplaces and rate lookups (Freightos, Searates type)Freight only. No Algerian duty or VAT, no DA, no deal-structure risk.
    Enterprise rate benchmarking (Xeneta type)Priced and built for large shippers, not a 50–500 person importer.
    Weekly indices (Drewry WCI, SCFI, FBX)Main trade lanes only. A number, not a decision.
    Your forwarder's quoteOne-sided. You cannot tell if it is fair.
