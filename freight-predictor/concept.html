You type in your shipment. The tool tells you what it will really cost delivered to your warehouse, when to book, whether your freight quote is fair, and whether your deal terms are risky.

Part 2 · Inside each engine

No magic. Four simple pieces of logic.

A

Cost engine

Adds every cost in the order customs sees them.

Goods + origin fees + freight + insurance = CIF value
Duty = duty % × CIF value
VAT = 19% × (CIF + duty) (paid, then usually recovered)
+ port fees + broker + truck + delay reserve
= Landed cost → × exchange rate → DA
B

Forecast engine

Freight is cheaper in some months and more expensive in others. Rates spike before Chinese New Year and during peak season.

Base price of the route × month factor = expected price
Range = expected ± 9%, wider every week further out
Price in 2 weeks higher by more than 3%? → Book now
Lower by more than 3%? → Wait
C

Quote checker

Places your forwarder's quote inside the expected range.

Below the range → Too cheap (something is hidden)
Low half of the range → Fair
High half of the range → Negotiable
Above the range → Overpriced
D

Risk rules

A list of "if this + that, then warn" rules, written from real import experience.

EXW + pay 100% upfront → Dangerous
CIF + T/T → Dangerous (supplier holds the B/L)
FOB + L/C at sight → Safe (gold standard)
Any "worst" flag decides the final verdict

Part 3 · How it is built

Three layers stacked on top of each other

This is the build-up. Each layer only talks to the one next to it, so we can upgrade one without breaking the others.

1 · Screen

What the user sees: the form on the left and the answers on the right. Built as a web page that can sit on your Webflow site.

2 · Logic

The four engines above. Plain formulas and rules. They take the inputs, read the data, and return the answers.

3 · Data

Freight price per route, month factors, duty rates, port fees, and the risk rules list. Today: tables we fill by hand. Later: weekly freight indices and real quotes from users. Upgrading this layer is what turns the calculator into a predictor.

    Part 5 · The plan

    Build it in three levels

    Level 1 · about 1 week

    Calculator

    Engines A, C and D with hand-filled data. Free on your site in exchange for an email. Every user is a future dashboard buyer.

    Level 2 · 4–8 weeks later

    Predictor

    Engine B with real weekly freight data. Paid monthly subscription.

    Level 3 · after ~200 quotes

    Price benchmark

    "Importers on your route paid $X last month." Built from users' real quotes. Nobody else has this data.
